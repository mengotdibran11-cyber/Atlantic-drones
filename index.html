Frequently Asked

Answers before you order

Do you ship across Canada?
Yes — free shipping across Canada on every airframe and accessory in the fleet, with tracked delivery on all orders.

Absolutely. Enterprise and fleet procurement enquiries include an on-site or in-store demo before you commit to an order.

Our certified technicians handle gimbal, motor, and sensor repairs for every airframe we sell, plus diagnostics on third-party units.

Enterprise and government fleet orders can be structured with staged payments — mention this in your enquiry and a specialist will follow up.
